StreamPulse bundled alert sounds

File sounds (public/sounds/<group>/*.mp3) are derived from Kenney audio packs,
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Converted to mono 96 kbps MP3. Attribution is not required.
  - Interface Sounds  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  - Digital Audio     https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
  - Impact Sounds     https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  - Music Jingles     https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles (Boyquotes/kenney-music-jingles-for-godot)
All clips loudness-normalized to about -14 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dB.
(Obtained via CC0 repackagings: github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds,
 Boyquotes/kenney-digital-audio-for-godot, Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot;
 each includes the CC0 1.0 licence text.)

Synthesized presets (builtin:zzfx_*) use a compact ZzFX-compatible generator.
