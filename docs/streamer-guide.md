# A smoother rhythm for your stream

StreamPulse is designed to support the show you already want to make. Plan what’s next, bring useful details into your scenes, and keep recurring production tasks close at hand.

Before going live, use Game Plan to gather your lineup and check that your scenes show the right information. During the broadcast, polls, alerts, chat commands, and a randomizer can each give an audience moment a clear place in the show. When it’s time to wrap, Credits Studio helps you recognize the people who joined in.

You don’t need to use everything at once. Start with one overlay or a simple schedule, then add tools when they solve a real problem in your routine. StreamPulse is a set of options for your production—not a checklist you have to complete.

Browse [features for your stream](features.md) or [get started](quickstart.md). If you’ve already installed StreamPulse, **Docs** and **API Reference** are available through the app navigation.
