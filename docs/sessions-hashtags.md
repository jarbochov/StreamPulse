# Sessions, Chat Search & Hashtag Tools

Review past streams, search across your entire chat archive, pin memorable moments, and moderate community hashtags.

## Chat Sessions & Global Search

Open **Library -> Sessions** (`/sessions.html`) to browse the current broadcast and all archived sessions:

- **Global Chat Search**: Search across every retained session at once using `AND`, `OR`, `"exact phrase"`, `user:name`, `#hashtag`, and wildcards (`*`), plus quick filter pills, scope/sort selectors, **Saved Searches** (create, edit, and delete reusable queries), and **Load More** pagination.
- **Shareable Links**: Search queries sync to `?q=` in the URL so you can bookmark or share filtered results.
- **Exports**: Download any session as **TSV**, **TXT**, **JSON**, or **PDF**.
- **Highlights** (`/highlights.html`): Pin notable chat messages during or after a stream for quick review.

## Hashtag Tools & Stats

- **Hashtag Stats** (`/hashtag-stats.html`): View all-time, 7/30/90-day, or current-session hashtag counts, unique days, and **Originator** attribution (the first observed chatter in retained `chat-*.json` / `chatlog-*.jsonl` logs when **Community insights** is enabled).
- **Manage Hashtags** (`/manage-hashtags.html`): Merge duplicate tags, rename tags, ban spam tags, or purge entries from stats and overlays.
- **Hashtags Overlay** (`/hashtags.html`): Show trending chat hashtags live in OBS.
