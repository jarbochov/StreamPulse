# Features for your show

Some nights need a polished scene. Others need a quick countdown or a way to let chat pick the next game. StreamPulse gives you room to do both, without needing to use every tool at once.

## Make the broadcast yours

**[Custom Overlay Studio](custom-overlays.md)** brings text, images, video, and live information into a visual editor. Keep starting, gameplay, and break layouts together with pages, or add a single element to your existing OBS scene.

**[Ready-to-use overlays](overlay-wizards.md)** get a timer, community goal, game schedule, or music display on screen without building a whole layout. Apple Music and VLC displays depend on your player and setup.

**[Promo Creator](promo-creator.md)** carries your channel's look beyond OBS. Make stream announcements in square, landscape, and portrait formats, with still-image and animated exports.

## Plan a night people can look forward to

**[Game Plan](game-plan-guide.md)** keeps tonight's lineup, future streams, and your backlog together. Show viewers what's playing now and what's coming next.

**[Timers and goals](overlay-wizards.md)** give a starting screen, break, challenge, or shared milestone a clear place in the show.

## Let your audience join in

**[Polls and tournaments](polls-guide.md)** turn decisions into live moments. Run audience votes, build a voting bracket, or control a competitive scoreboard with single- or double-elimination tournaments and round-robin standings.

**[Choice Randomizer](randomizer-guide.md)** reveals games, challenges, or participants through a wheel, roller, or card display. Prepare the list before the show and let the reveal have its moment.

**[Alerts](alerts-guide.md)** acknowledge support with visuals and sound. Pause, skip, or replay the queue when you need to keep the focus on the game or conversation.

**[Chat Bot](chatbot-guide.md)** shares helpful replies and reminders so routine questions don't pull you away from viewers.

## Remember the people and moments

**[Community Directory](community-guide.md)** brings recorded activity, notes, and tags together between broadcasts. These are private production tools, not something to display on stream.

**[Session review and Clips (Beta)](clips-beta.md)** help you revisit the night and consider moments worth sharing. Clip candidates need your review; a suggestion is not a published clip.

**[Credits Studio](credits-guide.md)** closes the night with supporter and chat activity, your own messages, and special thanks.

## Keep live controls within reach

Use OBS docks for your studios, or connect **[Bitfocus Companion](companion.md)** to put common actions on a Stream Deck.

The dashboard brings connection health and show activity together at a glance. It recovers when a connection returns, while hidden pages pause background status checks so an idle workspace does less work.

## Fits alongside the tools you already use

SocialStream Ninja handles unified chat, chat overlays, and featured messages. StreamPulse builds on that activity rather than replacing it. Twitch is our current focus; SSN's support for other platforms doesn't mean identical StreamPulse account features everywhere. Available events and actions depend on your connections and permissions.
