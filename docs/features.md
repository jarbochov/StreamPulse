# Features for your stream

StreamPulse helps you plan the show, put useful information on screen, involve your audience, and remember the people who joined you. Choose the tools that fit your broadcast; you do not need to use them all.

New here? Follow the [quick start](quickstart.md). For a complete stream routine, read the [streamer guide](streamer-guide.md).

## Alongside SocialStream Ninja, not instead of it

**SocialStream Ninja** provides unified chat, chat overlays, featured messages, and chat capture. Keep using it for those jobs.

**StreamPulse** builds on that activity with show planning, custom overlay layouts, timers and goals, audience randomizers, alerts, closing credits, and a local record of your broadcasts and community. Twitch account connections add Twitch-specific features such as subscriber and follower analytics.

**Our current focus is Twitch.** SocialStream Ninja supports other platforms, but that does not mean StreamPulse offers the same account integrations or feature coverage for them. Where the apps overlap, such as alerts or bot replies, choose which one should handle the interaction so viewers do not receive duplicates.

## Create a recognizable show

### Custom overlays

Build starting, gameplay, break, and closing layouts in a visual editor. Combine text, images, video, and live information, then adjust the look to match your channel. Use pages to keep related layouts together.

Automatic pages prioritize alerts, then featured messages, polls, and commands. If an alert interrupts your poll, the poll returns afterward while voting or its results are still current. Old messages and commands do not build up into a late replay queue.

**Good for:** a consistent stream identity without rebuilding each scene from scratch.

### Ready-to-use overlays

Use the overlay wizards to choose a look, preview it, and copy the link into OBS. Add countdowns, goals, recent activity, game schedules, or a now-playing display. Music displays support Apple Music and VLC; available features depend on your player and setup.

**Good for:** adding one useful element to an existing OBS scene.

### Promo Creator

Open **Tools > Promo Creator** to build announcements from shared Text, Image, and QR elements with independent square, landscape, and portrait placements. It includes reusable themes, 23 patterns with loopable motion, IGDB artwork, per-format typography and image crops, and all-format PNG ZIP or seamless MP4 export. Designs and custom themes save locally, and the editor reopens your last saved design.

**Good for:** a reusable “going live” image that matches your channel. See the [Promo Creator guide](promo-creator.md).

## Plan what comes next

### Game Plan

Keep tonight's lineup, upcoming games, backlog, and custom collections in one place. Schedule future streams and show viewers the current game or upcoming lineup. Update the plan as you move through the show.

**Good for:** variety streams, themed series, community nights, and keeping a backlog manageable.

### Timers and goals

Give viewers a clear countdown to your return, time an activity, or show progress toward a community goal. Match these displays to the rest of your overlay.

**Good for:** starting screens, breaks, challenges, and shared milestones.

## Make room for your community

### Choice Randomizer

Turn a list of games, challenges, or participants into a wheel, roller, or card reveal. Prepare choices before the stream, then reveal a result on screen when it is time for the audience moment.

**Good for:** choosing the next game, drawing a participant, or adding variety to a challenge.

### Live polls & Poll Studio

Run interactive audience votes directly from **Tools > Poll Studio**. StreamPulse supports both **Native Polls** with multi-platform chat voting (`!vote 1`, digits, keywords), Blind Mode ("Fog of War"), subscriber multipliers, victory fanfare, and confetti celebrations, as well as Twitch API polls displayed on screen. The Poll widget inside Custom Overlays adapts seamlessly to 2 to 5 choices with live countdown timers and winner highlights.

**Good for:** letting viewers decide what comes next, running challenges, and celebrating community decisions live on stream. See the [Live Polls guide](polls-guide.md).

### Versus Studio, Brackets & Matchups

Run community tournaments, esports showdowns, and fighting game match sets from **Tools > Versus Studio**. StreamPulse provides an interactive bracket controller (Single Elimination with automatic BYEs for non-power-of-2 entrant counts, Double Elimination, and Round Robin standings), plus a dedicated **Versus Scoreboard** (1v1, 3-way, 4-way FFA) with series win pips, side swapping (`P1 ⇄ P2`), Fight Card queues, and preset saving. Dedicated, self-sizing broadcast overlays (`/matchup.html` and `/tournament.html`) adapt seamlessly to any OBS browser source dimensions without letterboxing or fixed aspect ratio constraints.

**Good for:** fighting games, community tournaments, speedrun races, game night brackets, and esports score bugs. See the [Competitions & Polls guide](polls-guide.md).

### Alerts and the Alert Queue

Choose which events deserve an on-screen celebration and how they look and sound. Keep the queue beside your stream controls to pause, skip, or replay alerts. **Hide unmatched** helps you focus on events that triggered an alert rather than background activity.

**Good for:** acknowledging support without losing your place in the show. Available events depend on the platforms and connections you have enabled.

### Chat Bot

Give viewers helpful command replies, share recurring reminders, and keep a collection of quotes. Use permissions and cooldowns to keep automated replies useful rather than noisy. Chat replies need a working SocialStream Ninja connection.

**Good for:** answering common questions and sharing links while you focus on streaming.

### Community Directory

Look up recorded activity, keep notes and tags, and find regulars or newcomers. Use that context to make future conversations more personal, not to treat activity counts as a complete picture of a viewer.

**Good for:** remembering community connections between broadcasts. Keep notes and exported community information off your broadcast and share them carefully.

## Finish well and prepare for next time

### Credits Studio

Create an end-credits roll using available supporter and chat activity, with your own messages and special thanks. Choose sections, adjust the presentation, and preview your outro before showing it to viewers.

**Good for:** a closing moment that recognizes the people who helped make the stream.

### Session history and clip candidates

Review past sessions and chat when planning the next broadcast. The optional **Clips (Beta)** tools help identify moments to review; candidates are suggestions, not a guarantee that a clip has been created or published.

**Good for:** finding moments worth revisiting and carrying ideas into the next show.

### Hands-on controls

Keep controls in OBS docks or use Bitfocus Companion with a Stream Deck to put common actions within reach.

**Good for:** running credits, making audience choices, and managing live moments without hunting through windows.
