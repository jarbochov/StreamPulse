# Features for your show

Some nights need a polished scene. Others need a quick countdown or a way to let chat pick the next game. StreamPulse gives you room to do both, without needing to use every tool at once.

## Make the broadcast yours

**[Custom Overlay Studio](custom-overlays.md)** brings text, images, video, voice-reactive speaker badges, Element Style Presets, 31 animated borders, 31 procedural backgrounds, and live information into a visual editor. Keep starting, gameplay, and break layouts together with pages, or add a single element to your existing OBS scene.

**[Display Library](variable-displays.md)** lets you design reusable **Inventories**, **Tables**, **Member Badges & Strips**, **Counters & Pips**, **Meters & Gauges**, **News Tickers**, and **Bingo Cards** once, then place them across multiple Custom Overlays connected to independent live variables or Variable Set rosters.

**[Custom Variables & Variable Sets](variables-guide.md)** power live counters, selects, tags, dates, dynamic text templates, variable colors, and multi-field squad/guest rosters with rotating spotlights, per-owner progress trackers, and lazy direct item placeholders.

**Shared Color Palettes** centralize reusable 2–8 stop custom color palettes across Custom Overlay Studio (text, backgrounds, borders, and procedural patterns), OBS Pattern Studio, and OBS Border Studio so your channel branding stays synchronized everywhere.

**[OBS Studios (Border, Pattern & Layout)](obs-studios.md)** bring native GPU-rendered OBS Lua filters and sources directly into StreamPulse — design 31 hardware-accelerated source borders with Icon Borders and Transform Auto-Align, run 31 procedural pattern backgrounds natively on the OBS GPU, or rearrange scenes and morph source aspect ratios live with 9-point anchor pinning and Saved Layouts.

**[Ready-to-use overlays](overlay-wizards.md)** get a timer, community goal, game schedule, or music display on screen without building a whole layout. Apple Music and VLC displays depend on your player and setup.

**[Promo Creator](promo-creator.md)** (opt-in) carries your channel's look beyond OBS. Make stream announcements in square, landscape, and portrait formats, with still-image PNG and animated 6-second MP4 exports.

## Plan a night people can look forward to

**[Game Plan](game-plan-guide.md)** keeps tonight's lineup, future streams, and your backlog together. Show viewers what's playing now and what's coming next.

**[Timers, Goals & Subathon Automation](overlay-wizards.md)** give a starting screen, break, challenge, or shared milestone a clear place in the show. Enable **Subathon Automation** to extend countdown timers automatically from subs, bits, follows, raids, channel points, and donations with Boost Period multipliers, undo logs, and a dedicated Subathon Rules overlay.

## Let your audience join in

**[Polls and tournaments](polls-guide.md)** turn decisions into live moments. Run audience votes, build a voting bracket, or control a competitive scoreboard with single- or double-elimination tournaments and round-robin standings.

**[Tier List Maker](tier-list-guide.md)** runs interactive ranking shows with live chat consensus voting (`!tier`), lockable discussion pauses, reusable templates, multi-source item imports, and clean OBS overlays.

**[Interactive Trivia](trivia-guide.md)** brings television-grade quiz moments to your broadcast. Run 2 to 6 choice questions and True/False binary votes, drag-and-drop question reordering, played question tracking, progress countdown timers, live audience chat voting (`!a`, `!b`, `!true`, `!false`), distinct visual themes (including pure floating broadcast text), answer reveal fanfares, session & season leaderboards, and quiz pack presets.

**[Multiplayer Bingo (LAN)](multiplayer-bingo.md)** (opt-in) hosts local-network co-op and Lockout bingo races with saved Prompt Packs, auto-detected LAN player join links, optional Four Corners wins, Host Caller with Confirm Mode, and multi-view OBS overlays.

**[Choice Randomizer](randomizer-guide.md)** reveals games, challenges, or participants through a wheel, roller, or card display. Prepare the list before the show and let the reveal have its moment.

**[Alerts](alerts-guide.md)** acknowledge support with visuals and sound. Pause, skip, mute, or replay the queue when you need to keep the focus on the game or conversation.

**[Chat Bot](chatbot-guide.md)** shares helpful replies and reminders so routine questions don't pull you away from viewers.

## Remember the people and moments

**[Community Directory](community-guide.md)** brings recorded activity, notes, and tags together between broadcasts. These are private production tools, not something to display on stream.

**[Session review and Clips (Beta)](clips-beta.md)** help you revisit the night and consider moments worth sharing. Clip candidates need your review; a suggestion is not a published clip.

**[Credits Studio](credits-guide.md)** closes the night with supporter and chat activity, your own messages, and special thanks.

## Keep live controls within reach

**[Operator Mode](operator-guide.md)** brings your live broadcast controls into a single customizable multi-column Live Board and tabbed control room — built for desktop monitors, tablets (with Touch Density), or narrow OBS Custom Browser Docks, complete with named Presets, Show Mode safety locks, and server-saved Markdown Runbooks.

Use OBS docks for your studios, or connect **[Bitfocus Companion](companion.md)** to put common actions on a Stream Deck.

The dashboard brings connection health and show activity together at a glance. When the global OBS connection is enabled, **Health** shows whether OBS is connected, connecting, or disconnected, independently of the Scene Sync module. It recovers when a connection returns, while hidden pages pause background status checks so an idle workspace does less work.

## Fits alongside the tools you already use

SocialStream Ninja handles unified chat, chat overlays, and featured messages. StreamPulse builds on that activity rather than replacing it. Twitch is our current focus; SSN's support for other platforms doesn't mean identical StreamPulse account features everywhere. Available events and actions depend on your connections and permissions.
