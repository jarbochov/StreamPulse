# When something doesn’t look right

Most StreamPulse setup hiccups come down to a connection or a scene that needs refreshing. If chat activity isn’t appearing, first check that SocialStream Ninja is open and that StreamPulse shows its connection. If an OBS browser source looks blank or out of date, confirm StreamPulse is running and refresh that source.

For features that depend on Twitch, check that the right account is connected. If you have just changed permissions, reconnecting may be needed. When a control or display behaves differently than expected, try it in the relevant StreamPulse studio before changing your live scene.

The [quick start](quickstart.md) covers the initial setup, and the [feature overview](features.md) can help you find the right tool. For more help, use the support links on the site.

Already installed? Open **Docs** and **API Reference** from the app navigation for product and technical details.
