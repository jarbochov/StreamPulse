# An independent project, built for your show

StreamPulse is free and open source. It's made to give streamers a creative workspace they can run on their own computer, with no StreamPulse subscription or hosted account.

## Have a question or hit a snag?

Already using StreamPulse? Open **Docs** in the app for setup and studio instructions. **API Reference** is there too if you're connecting custom controls or automations.

For a bug or feature idea, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/jarbochov/StreamPulse/issues). Tell us what you expected, what happened, and which version you're using. A clear example makes it easier to help. Remove account details, tokens, real chat, and private community notes from screenshots or logs before sharing.

There's no dedicated support team or guaranteed response time. Reports from people using StreamPulse help make the project better.

## Help keep it growing

If StreamPulse helps your broadcast, you can [support development on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jarbochov). You can also help by reporting a reproducible issue, improving the project, or sharing what you've made.

Want to contribute? The [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jarbochov/StreamPulse) has the source and contribution information. The project is available under the [MIT License](https://github.com/jarbochov/StreamPulse/blob/main/LICENSE).
