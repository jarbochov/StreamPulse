# Put stream controls within reach

Connect StreamPulse with Bitfocus Companion to bring common broadcast actions to a Stream Deck or another Companion surface. Instead of switching windows to find a control, trigger it from the place you already manage your show.

Use buttons for practical moments such as advancing Game Plan, starting a randomizer reveal, managing a poll, or controlling a timer. Companion feedback can also help show what’s active, so a control surface can become a useful part of your production workspace.

StreamPulse has a dedicated Companion module maintained separately from the main app. See the [StreamPulse Companion module](https://github.com/jarbochov/companion-module-streampulse) for its current actions and setup details.

Explore [StreamPulse features](features.md) or [get started](quickstart.md). If StreamPulse is already installed, **Docs** and **API Reference** are available in the app navigation.
