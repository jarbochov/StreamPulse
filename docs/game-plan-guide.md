# Keep the next chapter of your stream in view

Game Plan gives your broadcasts a flexible rundown. Keep tonight’s lineup close, save future ideas for later, and see how a game moves from “maybe” to “playing now.”

![Game Plan Studio with a fictional schedule](images/game-plan-studio.png)

Use the calendar to plan upcoming streams, pin games to **Tonight's Lineup** without removing them from their home list (`Schedule`, `Up Next`, or custom collections), group titles into flexible horizon blocks (like `Next Week` or `This Month`), and keep a backlog ready for the next time you need inspiration. Multi-select rows for bulk moves, sync unsynced schedule slots to Twitch in one pass, or export and import your plan anytime.

## Share the lineup on stream

When you want viewers in on the plan, add a live Game Plan overlay to your OBS scene. Switch between a full **Schedule**, a bottom **Ticker Bar**, or a compact **Checklist** view — artwork helps make every title recognizable at a glance.
