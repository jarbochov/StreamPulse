## Choose the app for your computer

**macOS:** disk images and ZIP downloads are available for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Choose the build that matches your Mac.

**Windows:** choose the setup installer, or the portable app if you prefer not to install it.

The release page lists the version, download files, and release notes. **Source-code ZIP files are not desktop installers.**

**The apps are currently unsigned.** Your operating system may show a warning. Download only from the official repository, review the release, and proceed only if you trust it. Don't disable your system's security protections.

## What you'll use alongside it

**OBS Studio** displays your overlays. **SocialStream Ninja** brings in chat and supported events. An optional **Twitch connection** unlocks account-specific features, and **Bitfocus Companion** adds hardware controls.

StreamPulse runs on your computer and needs to stay running while OBS uses its overlays. Connected services need internet access; your saved workspace remains local.

## Open the app and start small

Pick one overlay, preview it, and try it in OBS before going live. The app's **Docs** includes setup and studio instructions, and **API Reference** covers automation. This website is a tour of what you can create, not a second instruction manual.

Updating an existing installation? Back up first and quit the app fully while off-air before replacing it. Closing the window keeps StreamPulse running in the background.
