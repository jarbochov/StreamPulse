# Turn a decision into a shared moment

Polls, tournament brackets, and head-to-head match scoreboards bring audience participation straight into the show. Put a choice to chat, follow the vote as it develops, and reveal the result in a broadcast-ready display.

## Audience Polls

Use a poll for a quick decision — what to play, which challenge to try, or what happens next. Save reusable prompt packs in the Poll Library and preview the live OBS bar graphic before opening voting in chat.

## Brackets &amp; Versus Scoreboards

When you want a multi-round event, create a tournament bracket and let matches build toward a champion — or use **Versus** when you need a dedicated head-to-head scoreboard with match timers, rounds, and queued challengers.

Keep a bracket ready for another night with reusable templates, or save a checkpoint to return to its progress later. Blind votes save the surprise for the end of a match, double elimination supports full Grand Finals resets, and round-robin standings adjust cleanly when match results are updated.
